Before you head out

A few things
you might wonder.

Where is Musafir Travel located?

Find us at Nepali Farm, Rishikesh. Call +91 78954 01724 for the exact meeting point and pickup directions.

What do I need to rent a bike?

Ask the team to confirm the required driving licence, identity documents, deposit, fuel policy and rental terms before booking.

Are helmets and pickup included?

A free helmet is included with every rental, as advertised. Pickup and drop are available; confirm the area, timing and any charges with the team.

Can I book rafting or bungee jumping here?

Yes, you can send an enquiry. The team will confirm the operator, availability, price, eligibility and safety requirements. Activities may depend on weather and operating conditions.

Does an online enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Your booking is confirmed only when the Musafir Travel team confirms availability, price and booking terms directly with you.
